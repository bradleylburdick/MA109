    1.

    Write each of the following in interval notation (Note: you may find it helpful to draw a number line):
    1. \(\displaystyle x \ge -5\)
      🔗
    2. \(x \le -3\) or \(x \gt 6\)
      🔗
    3. \(\displaystyle x \neq 88\)
      🔗
    4. \(x \ge -2\) and \(x \lt 6\)
      🔗
    5. Everything except \(\pm 6\)
      🔗
    6. \(\displaystyle x \lt 18\)
      🔗
    7. Everything except \(0\)
      🔗
    8. \(x \lt -5\) or \(x \gt -5\)
      🔗
    🔗
    Answer.
    1. \(\displaystyle [-5, \infty)\)
      🔗
    2. \(\displaystyle (-\infty,-3]\cup(6,\infty)\)
      🔗
    3. \(\displaystyle (-\infty,88)\cup(88,\infty)\)
      🔗
    4. \(\displaystyle [-2, 6)\)
      🔗
    5. \(\displaystyle (-\infty,-6)\cup(-6,6)\cup(6,\infty)\)
      🔗
    6. \(\displaystyle (-\infty,18)\)
      🔗
    7. \(\displaystyle (-\infty,0)\cup(0,\infty)\)
      🔗
    8. \(\displaystyle (-\infty, -5)\cup(-5, \infty)\)
      🔗
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗
